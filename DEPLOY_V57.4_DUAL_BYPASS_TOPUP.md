# TeoStudio V57.4 — Dual Free bypass + standalone top-up

## Free access flow
- First eligible Free bypass step uses BumX (`BUMX_API_URL`, `BUMX_API_KEY`).
- One key is generated by TeoStudio after return to `/free-key.html`; it is single-use, stored as a hash, bound to the Studio session, and expires after 5 minutes. Copy success hides the displayed key.
- Redeeming BumX records step 1/2. Link4M is only available after at least 24 hours.
- Redeeming Link4M step 2 grants 7 days of Free access and resets the cycle. Active rental time skips the bypass gate.
- A legacy V57.3 account already at step 1 receives a one-time migration anchor 24 hours in the past to avoid being stuck.

## Cloudflare variables
Already present in the supplied Production screenshot; keep these values: `BUMX_API_URL`, Secret `BUMX_API_KEY`, `LINK4M_API_URL`, Secret `LINK4M_API_KEY`.

Add `LINK4M_REWARDED_TRAFFIC_APPROVED=1` only after Link4M has explicitly granted written permission for incentivized traffic. Link4M's public terms require account holders to be at least 18 and prohibit incentivized traffic unless specially permitted. The intended 7-day reward is incentivized traffic. The publisher/account should therefore be operated by an eligible adult, and step 2 must remain disabled until Link4M grants explicit written permission; do not work around the provider's policy.

No `FREE_BYPASS_VERIFY_URL`, `FREE_BYPASS_API_KEY`, or `FREE_BYPASS_REWARDED_TRAFFIC_APPROVED` is used by this release. Remove obsolete variables only after Production has deployed this version and you confirmed no old Worker revision needs them.

## Card top-up page
- New standalone page: `/topup.html?method=card` (bank transfer remains available as a tab).
- Backend only credits wallet after TheCaoSieuToc provider reports/validates success. Duplicate card fingerprints, rate limits, Turnstile, verified email, and callback signature checks remain.
- Add Cloudflare Secrets `THECAOSIEUTOC_PARTNER_ID` and `THECAOSIEUTOC_PARTNER_KEY` from your TheCaoSieuToc partner account. They were not visible in the supplied Cloudflare screenshot. Do not put card-provider secrets in frontend code.
- Both card and bank top-ups require an acknowledgement that a parent/guardian has agreed when the customer is under 18. This is self-attestation only, not age verification; an adult should manage merchant/payment-provider obligations and handle disputes/refunds.

## Ephemeral key cleanup
- The Worker scheduled handler purges expired/unredeemed key hashes and expired attempts every 5 minutes (`*/5 * * * *`), while the full maintenance sweep remains hourly (`0 * * * *`). It does not store plaintext keys in D1.
- The Cloudflare scheduled trigger must be deployed with the Worker for automatic cleanup to run.
